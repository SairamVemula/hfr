# Code of conduct

We want HFR to be a friendly, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing other people's private information, or sexualised language or imagery.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's Security tab or the email on the maintainers' GitHub profiles. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
